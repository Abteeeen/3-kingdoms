## The pitch in five lines

1. **No website.** The mall listing is the only page about them. It shows an hours table, no menu and no food.
2. **Every online order pays a marketplace.** App prices are 22–34% above in-store, and there is no direct pickup option.
3. **"Hard to find" is the top review theme**, and Google lists the wrong floor.
4. **Rivals in the same building.** Zhang Liang Malatang (same level, open until 10pm) and David's Noodle & Hot Pot (Level E) sell the same spicy build-your-own bowl.
5. **No Brisbane rival has a Chinese-language menu.** A bilingual site would be first.
