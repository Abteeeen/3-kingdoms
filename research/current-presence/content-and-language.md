## 4. Product format (from menus, reviews and creators)

- **Mini hotpot / mao cai with rice:** one bowl of protein, vegetables and noodles in Sichuan broth, served with steamed rice. Standard vegetables: "wombok, sugarloaf, broccoli, cauliflower" (Uber/DoorDash); a reviewer also lists daikon (DoorDash, Jul 2023).
- **Special / Signature Mini Hotpot:** "your choice of three protein: Beef, Lamb, Fish, Chicken, Coriander Pork Meatballs, Seafood Sticks, Prawn, Chinese Sausage, Luncheon Meat" (Uber). Ritual (legacy) called it "Special Mao Cai … your choice of 3 protein".
- **DIY "Mini Hotpot of Your Choice":** 500g ($26.80) or 1000g ($53.50) on HungryPanda, "rice not included". A creator says "Custom Hot Pot 500g (4-6 options)". Creators cite "40" and "50+" ingredients.
- **Spice levels:** "6 spice levels" (creator @foodbaby_ang). Range "from no spice to extra plus" (creator). Menu text says "from not spicy to extra hot". Google reviewer: medium was "definitely on the spicy side".
- **Noodle choices** (HungryPanda add-ons and creators): white egg noodles, ramen, rice noodles, thin sweet potato noodles, flat sweet potato noodles, udon, Sichuan noodles, rice vermicelli, konjac (creator mention). The kitchen claims "handmade noodles".
- **Allergen note:** most items say "May contain nuts" or "Contains nuts" (Special Mini Hotpot: "Contains nuts").
- **Service:** "You order at the counter and pick up your food when it’s ready. … Utensils are self serve and water is also available near the counter." (Google, Lynn)
- **New item:** Signature Sticky Rice (Instagram, Aug 2026). It is not on any delivery menu yet.

### Themes
**Praise:** broth and mala/numbing flavour ("best Mala base in brisbane"); braised and marinated beef noodle soups; customisation and choice; value, especially with the EatClub discount; friendly, patient staff; convenient spot by the bus stops; Mandarin-speaking staff.
**Complaints:** portion size versus price, especially add-ons and sides; inconsistent spice level (hotter than ordered); salty broth; wrong order; missing utensils on delivery; dark, red, sometimes empty room; hard to find.
