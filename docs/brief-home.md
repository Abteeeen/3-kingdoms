## 1. One offer, one audience, one action

- **Offer:** a Sichuan mao cai bowl built your way, at counter prices: Signature $20.80, against $25.80–$27.80 on the delivery apps.
- **Audience:** CBD lunch crowd, students and creators' followers, on phones, in or near Uptown.
- **Primary action:** order pickup.
  - **Until a provider is live,** the button reads **"Build your order"**. It opens the menu, and you build a ticket to show at the counter. We must not say "order ahead" before it is possible (`landing-page-design` A5: the CTA says what you get).
  - **When a provider is chosen,** the same button becomes **"Order pickup"** and goes to checkout.
- **Second action:** "Find us". It never competes visually with the primary.

## 2. Layout

**A, classic hero plus sections**, told as one tall poster: every section is a band, and the eye reads top to bottom through ranked type. Option B (long-form story) would bury the price and the location. The visitor needs three facts fast: what it is, what it costs, where it is.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The home page is a broadside for one bowl. Stacked full-width bands of wood type, ranked by importance, state the name, the counter price and the shop number. It refuses the category default: a food photo with an "Order now" button and a row of menu cards.

**OWN-WORLD.**
- **Palette:** kraft `#C9A57B` ground, carbon ink `#1C1A17` type and rules, chilli red `#D7261E` as an overprint run for the one headline band and the primary action, peppercorn green `#6F8A2A` as a second run slightly off-register for mild heat and greens, and ticket white `#FBFAF7` only for the order ticket and menu slips.
- **Type:** Big Shoulders Display for wood-type bands, ZCOOL QingKe HuangYou for Chinese bands, Zilla Slab for text.
- **States are ink treatments:** active is a solid overprint, disabled is a dry ghost impression, and emphasis is a second off-register run.
- **Shape:** square corners, with rules between bands.

**STORY.** In one screen the visitor learns three things: what it is (Sichuan mini hotpot, built your way), what it costs ($20.80 at the counter, less than the apps), and where it is (Shop 32, Level A, open till 7). They believe it because the prices and hours are exact and three real reviews back it up. Then they build an order, or find the shop.
